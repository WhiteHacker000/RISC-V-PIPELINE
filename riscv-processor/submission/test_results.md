cycles/retired and retired/cycles. WB completion matched retirement in every
row.

| Branch-heavy loop | 25 | 13 / 13 | 0 | 4 | 1.923 | 0.520 |

The longer independent streams amortize the four-cycle finite-pipeline
fill/drain overhead. Forwarding allows the dependent arithmetic chain to avoid
load-use stalls. The load-use case incurs one interlock; taken branches redirect
in EX and flush wrong-path work. These are simulation CPI/IPC measurements, not
hardware speedup, clock frequency, execution time or FPGA timing.
